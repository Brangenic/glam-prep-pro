Carnival Glam Hub was co-founded in 2017 by Kibwe McGann and Gabrielle Waite.

Rules:
- Neither person is ever "the founder" of Carnival Glam Hub. Both are co-founders, hyphenated, British form.
- When named as a pair, Kibwe McGann comes first.
- "founded in 2017 by Kibwe McGann and Gabrielle Waite" is acceptable because both are named. A single name attached to "founded" is not.
- Gabrielle Waite is the sole founder of Gabby Glam Cosmetics and of Glam Haus by Gabby Glam. Never weaken those to co-founder.
- Kibwe McGann is never a "booking director".
- Exception: a press headline or a direct quotation from an article is that outlet's words and is never edited, even where it says founder.

**Why:** Kibwe's standing rule, 14 September 2026. Enforced in part by `src/test/prerenderDrift.test.ts`.
