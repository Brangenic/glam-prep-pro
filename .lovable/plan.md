

# Carnival Glam Hub — Premium One-Page Landing Page

## Overview
A high-converting, single-page luxury landing page for Carnival Glam Hub — a premium carnival morning preparation service. The design follows a "Dusk to Dawn" dark theme with bold typography (Syne + Inter), cinematic scroll experience, and mobile-first conversion focus.

## Design System
- **Background:** Deep Onyx (#101010) — premium, immersive dark canvas
- **Primary CTA:** Solar Flare (#FF5C00) — urgent, energetic orange-red
- **Secondary Accent:** Electric Orchid (#DA70D6) — feminine magenta for hover states & highlights
- **Text:** Alabaster (#F2F2F2) — soft off-white for readability
- **Typography:** Syne (Bold 800) for headlines, Inter for body/UI
- **No carousels, no generic icons, no before/after sliders**

## Sections (12 total, scroll-based funnel)

### 1. Hero — "Your Carnival Morning Starts Here"
Full-screen dark hero with bold Syne headline, subheadline in Inter, and ONE primary CTA: **"Choose Your Destination"**. Premium carnival imagery as background with subtle gradient overlay. No secondary CTA — singular focus drives confidence.

### 2. Social Proof Strip
Horizontal bar with 4 credibility stats: "13,000+ Masqueraders Since 2017" · "Trusted by Carnival Bands" · "Professional Glam Artists" · "Premium Experience". Compact, high-impact, uses Alabaster text with subtle dividers.

### 3. The Problem — "Carnival Morning Should Feel Exciting — Not Stressful"
Compressed, text-focused section with relatable pain points (early appointments, rushing, sweating before the road). Emotionally engaging typography treatment — each pain point as a short, punchy line. Dark, tense energy. Transition line pulls into the solution.

### 4. The Glam Hub Experience (Solution)
**Signature "Glam Reveal" moment** — as user scrolls past the problem, a full-screen glamorous portrait wipes vertically upward (scroll-linked animation). Section introduces Glam Hub as a complete carnival morning experience, not just makeup. Clean, confident copy in Inter with Syne headline.

### 5. Services Section
No icons — bold Syne service titles with concise descriptions. Modular blocks for: Makeup, Hair Styling, Costume Dressing, Content & Photos, Concierge Prep. Each block uses subtle borders and hover states in Electric Orchid. Clean grid, not crowded.

### 6. Destination Booking — Full-Screen Takeover
**Convention break:** Background fades to pure black. Two massive cinematic destination cards animate into view — Jamaica and Saint Lucia — plus an "Upcoming Destinations" card. Each card is large, image-driven, with destination name in Syne and a Solar Flare CTA button. Feels like a moment of decision.

### 7. Gallery — Editorial Visual Grid
Full-bleed, asymmetric photo grid showing final glam results, artists at work, carnival-ready clients. No captions cluttering the imagery. Feels editorial and aspirational — the "exhale" in the compress-and-release rhythm.

### 8. Testimonials — "What Masqueraders Are Saying"
3 testimonial cards on dark background. Electric Orchid oversized quote marks. Client name and carnival reference. Credibility stats (Google reviews, years active) displayed alongside. No slider — all visible.

### 9. Founder Story — Full-Width Immersive
Full-width atmospheric image of the founder in their element. Brand story overlaid as clean typographic treatment — not boxed beside a photo. References founding in 2017, Gabby Glam, and the mission. Feels integrated and credible.

### 10. FAQ Section
Clean accordion-style FAQ. 5-6 key booking questions. Minimal design — Syne for questions, Inter for answers. Reduces friction before final CTA.

### 11. Final CTA — "Secure Your Carnival Glam Slot"
Dark section with urgency messaging about limited slots. Large Syne headline. Single Solar Flare CTA button: **"Book Your Glam Appointment"**. This is the strongest conversion point on the page.

### 12. Footer
Minimal: contact email, phone, Instagram, policy links. Optional newsletter signup. No clutter or duplication.

## Mobile UX
- **Sticky booking button** fixed to bottom of screen on mobile
- Smooth anchor scroll navigation
- All sections stack cleanly on mobile
- Touch-friendly destination cards
- Fast-loading optimized gallery

## Technical Approach
- All sections built as React components with Tailwind styling
- Scroll-linked animations using CSS/intersection observers
- Smooth anchor navigation between sections
- Responsive mobile-first layout
- Custom Google Fonts (Syne + Inter) loaded via index.html
- All content is static/hardcoded (no backend needed)
