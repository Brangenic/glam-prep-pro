# Carnival Glam Concierge

Carnival Glam Hub



You are acting as a Senior UI/UX Designer, Conversion Strategist, and Brand Experience Designer.



Rebuild the entire Carnival Glam Hub website as a single high-converting landing page that feels premium, feminine, energetic, and luxury-service-led while still being easy to use on mobile.



This is not a basic “compress everything onto one page” task.

This is a full one-page conversion rebuild with a strong information hierarchy, smoother storytelling, cleaner sections, and a more polished booking journey.





Primary Goal





Drive users to book their glam package / choose their destination.





Secondary Goals





Build trust quickly

Showcase the full luxury concierge-style experience

Make destination-based booking feel easy

Highlight social proof, founder credibility, and service value

Reduce clutter and remove weak or distracting elements









Current Site Context to Improve





The current site includes:



A hero section with the headline “Get Carnival-Ready with Glam Hub” and destination-based CTAs. 

Messaging around Carnival prep stress and Glam Hub as the solution. 

Reviews, Amazon finds, multiple CTAs, a gallery section, and contact/footer information. 

Multiple menu items and extra links that make the experience feel more fragmented than necessary for a one-page conversion site. 

A visible broken widget/error state that should be fully removed from the new experience. 





The rebuild should preserve the brand’s energy and service offering, but present it in a much cleaner, more premium, and more strategic way.







One-Page Structure Required







1. Hero Section





Design a premium hero section that immediately communicates:



What Carnival Glam Hub is

Who it is for

Why it matters

What the user should do next





Include:



Strong headline

Clear subheadline

Primary CTA: Book / Choose Your Destination

Secondary CTA: View Services or See Packages

Premium imagery that feels modern, editorial, and Carnival-luxury focused





This section should feel high-trust and visually elevated.







2. The Problem / The Reality of Carnival Morning





Create a short section that captures the chaos of Carnival prep without Glam Hub:



Early appointments

Running around town

Last-minute stress

Costume dressing confusion

Sweating before hitting the road





This should be visually engaging and emotionally relatable, but more polished and intentional than the current version.







3. The Solution — What Glam Hub Actually Offers





Create a section that clearly explains the Glam Hub value proposition:



Makeup

Hair

Costume dressing assistance

Photos / content moments

Concierge-style prep experience

Destination-based glam support





This section should make it feel like Glam Hub is not just a beauty service, but a premium Carnival readiness experience.







4. Services / Packages Overview





Create a visually strong services section with cards or modular blocks for:



Makeup

Hair

Costume Dressing

Photos / Content Add-ons

Concierge / Premium Experience

Destination-specific availability





Do not overcrowd the page with too many details.

Focus on clarity, desirability, and conversion.







5. Destination Booking Section





Build a dedicated destination section that makes it easy for users to choose where they’re booking for.



Destinations currently referenced include Jamaica and Saint Lucia, and the site also references broader glam destinations in the footer. Keep this section visually organized and easy to scan. 



This section should:



Feel important

Use destination cards or tiles

Lead directly into booking / package selection









6. Social Proof / Reviews





Rebuild the reviews section into a polished testimonial block that feels trustworthy and premium.



Current site includes review content and mentions strong reputation signals such as Google reviews and “13,000+ Masqueraders since 2017.” Preserve credibility signals like these, but present them in a more elevated and readable way. 



Include:



Testimonial cards or slider

Star rating / credibility stats

Optional founder credibility









7. Gallery / Experience Section





Create a premium photo-led section showing the Glam Hub experience:



Final glam results

Carnival-ready looks

Behind-the-scenes luxury prep energy

Client moments





This should feel editorial and aspirational, not like a cluttered image dump.







8. Founder / Brand Credibility





Create a section introducing the brand story and credibility.



Current site states the brand was founded in 2017 and references Gabby Glam and Kibwe McGann. Retain founder credibility, but present it more cleanly and intentionally. 



This section should reinforce:



Experience

Professionalism

Trust

Industry credibility









9. FAQs





Add a short, clean FAQ section answering the most important booking questions:



How do I book?

Which destinations are available?

What is included?

How far in advance should I reserve?

Do slots sell out?

What happens after booking?





This should reduce friction before the final CTA.







10. Final CTA Section





Create a strong closing section that drives urgency and booking action.



Focus on:



Limited availability

Carnival dates approaching

Premium experience

Simple call to action





Primary CTA should be the strongest conversion point on the page.







11. Footer





Simplify the footer.



Keep only what supports trust and action:



Contact number

Email

Social links

Key policy links

Mailing address if needed

Optional newsletter signup only if it feels intentional





Do not overload the footer with unnecessary duplication.

Premium

Luxurious but still vibrant





Avoid:

Generic beauty-site aesthetics





The site should feel like a premium Carnival concierge beauty brand, not just a salon landing page.
