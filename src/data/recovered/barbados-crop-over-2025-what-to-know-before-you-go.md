### 🛬 Touchdown Tips: Flights, Cash & Getting Connected
You'll be landing at **Grantley Adams International Airport (BGI)**. Book flights early, Crop Over is high-demand, especially for UK and Canadian girlies.
- **Currency:** The Barbadian Dollar (BBD) is typically 2:1 for USD, ~2.5:1 for GBP, ~2.2:1 for EUR, ~1.5:1 for CAD. Hit the **airport cambio** for safe exchanges; around \$300 BBD should hold you.
- **Get connected:** Buy an **eSIM** before you land (Airalo or Holafly). Roaming? We don't do that here.
- **Transport:** No Uber, but plenty of **ZR vans** and taxis, or rent a pastel **MOKE car** for the week. Super Instagrammable.

### 🏠 Where to Drop Your Bags
- **The Gap (St. Lawrence Gap)**, heart of the nightlife, walkable to food, beach and bars.
- **Rockley/Accra Beach**, chill vibes, good beach access.
- **Oistins**, more local, with that Friday Fish Fry energy.

### 🍴 What to Eat (And Where to Find It)
- **National Dish:** Cou Cou and Flying Fish.
- **Must-do:** **Oistins Fish Fry** on Friday night, seafood grilled fresh, live music, vibes for days.
- **Late-night eats:** **St. Lawrence Gap** for jerk, doubles, and grilled chicken.
- **Cheffette:** fast food chain but a local rite of passage. Their rotis? Bless.
- **Mount Gay & Cockspur Rum:** local and legendary. Mount Gay even has a distillery tour.

### 🎉 Crop Over: Events You Don't Wanna Miss
- **Foreday Morning**, their version of J'ouvert: paint, mud, soca at sunrise.
- **Kadooment Day**, Crop Over's main parade. Costumes, feathers, soca, and sun. A cultural reset.

### 🐚 Beyond the Road: Explore Barbados
- **Harrison's Cave**, underground beauty.
- **Animal Flower Cave**, coastal wonder with natural pools.
- **Watch for monkeys**, wild ones roam, and yes, they're cute from a distance.
- **Rihanna Drive**, Queen Rih has her own street. Worth the detour.
- **Accra Beach**, great for sunbathing or a morning sea dip.
