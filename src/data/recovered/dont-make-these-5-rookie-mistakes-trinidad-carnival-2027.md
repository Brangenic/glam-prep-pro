Hey Carnival newbie, so you're venturing into Trinidad for Carnival 2027. Exciting! But trust me, I've been there, and there are pitfalls to avoid. Here's your quick guide.

**1. Shoe choice is everything.** Trinidad Carnival is a fete marathon and your feet will feel it. Say no to new shoes. Opt for comfy open slippers or wedges, break them in weeks before, band-aid your toes, and thank me later.

**2. Bag drama? No thanks.** Crime exists everywhere, so ditch the flashy handbags. A slim wallet, fanny pack or thigh pack is your friend. Keep cash modest, around $300 to $400 TT (about US$40 to 50). Download [TT Ride Share](https://ttrideshare.com/) for safe, card-friendly taxis.

**3. Headgear rule: keep it on.** If your costume comes with headgear, don't take it off mid-march. It messes with your makeup, and we're here for the Instagrammable moments. Embrace the tan line, it's a Carnival souvenir.

**4. Wings come with scars.** Big backpacks or wings? Expect battle scars, it's a rite of passage. The simple rule: the bigger the wings, the deeper the scars, the better-looking your photos.

**5. Avoid the vendor chase.** Carnival morning chaos awaits if you book different vendors in different places. Between traffic, road closures and crazy one-ways, splitting your services across town is a sure way to ruin your morning.
