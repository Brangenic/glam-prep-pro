### According to GPT, here are the top 5 Caribbean Carnivals in reverse order, with my personal take on each

### 5. Saint Lucia Carnival, Vibes & Beauty 🌴🎶
**Dates for 2025:** July 14–15, 2025

**My Carnival Experience:** Saint Lucia might be small, but its Carnival brings big vibes. What makes it unique is that they proudly showcase their own brand of Soca, so get ready to learn new lyrics. Bum Bum Wall is a must. It's two full days of Carnival with one of the longest routes, so prepare to be on your feet and dancing for hours. And girl, they don't stop for lunch, it's a rolling eat-and-go situation. I'd recommend sneakers for the road; they have no carts and nobody on top of trucks.

**Fetes to check out:** Colour, Me Red, U4RIA, Rise.

**Things to Do:** Hike the **Piton mountains** or soak in the **sulfur springs** after all that dancing. The beaches are pristine.

**Travel Hack:** Stay along Rodney Bay or Gros Islet. Anywhere outside that makes transport hell and you'll be stuck in traffic for hours.

### 4. Grenada's Spicemas, Raw & Unfiltered 🖤💥
**Dates for 2025:** August 11–12, 2025

**Carnival Experience:** In Grenada's Spicemas, seeing people parading with devil horns and covered in oil, paint and mud is completely normal. This is the heart of **Jab Jab**, a tradition steeped in Grenadian history, originating from enslaved Africans who used it as a form of resistance. It's raw and intense, unlike any other Carnival. The road is a vibe, the costumes are fire, but it's Jab Jab the day before for me.

**Fetes to check out:** Soca Monarch, Fancy Mas Parade.

**Things to Do:** Visit the **underwater sculpture park** or grab nutmeg and cinnamon at the spice markets.

**Travel Hack:** The men get a little handsy during Jab Jab, make sure you're with a crew.

### 3. Jamaica Carnival, Cool Vibes, Hot Energy 🇯🇲🔥
**Dates for 2025:** April 27, 2025

**Carnival Experience:** Jamaica's Carnival is one of the most fun and energetic experiences going. The mix of Soca and Dancehall creates a unique, high-energy vibe. The road march is a pure party from morning to night.

**Fetes to check out:** Immortal J'ouvert, I Love Soca, Sunnation Breakfast Party.

**Things to Do:** Head to **Negril** for white sand, **Ocho Rios** for **Dunn's River Falls**, or **Portland** for an off-the-beaten-path experience.

**Travel Hack:** Stay in the New Kingston area; it's worth renting a car.

### 2. Barbados Crop Over, Glam & Culture 🇧🇧✨
**Carnival Experience:** Crop Over is where culture meets glam in the most fabulous way. It's all about the glitz and the infectious energy of Grand Kadooment Day. The costumes are beautiful and the vibes don't stop.

**Fetes to check out:** Caesar's Army A.M.Bush, Bliss, Foreday Morning Jam.

**Things to Do:** Visit **Bridgetown** (a UNESCO World Heritage site), chill at **Crane or Accra Beach**, and try the Bajan staple, **flying fish and cou-cou**.

### 1. Trinidad & Tobago Carnival, The Mecca of Carnival 🇹🇹🎉
**Dates for 2025:** March 3–4, 2025

**Carnival Experience:** This is the big one. Trinidad Carnival is the gold standard worldwide, and for good reason. From the epic two days of road march to the non-stop fetes leading up to it, the energy is unmatched. Costumes are next-level, the Soca is top-tier, and nothing compares to the sheer size of it. It's beautiful chaos.

**Fetes to check out:** Soca Brainwash, Stink and Dutty, Jumbie J'ouvert, Sunnation Breakfast Party.

**Things to Do:** Head **Down the Islands (DDI)** for a boat party, hop over to **Tobago** for beaches, and grab **Bake and Shark** at **Maracas Beach**. Doubles are a must.

**Travel Hack:** Buy most things a year in advance and party tickets at least 6 months before. Prices get crazy otherwise.

- Bring a sewing kit, boob tape, nipple covers and band-aids. Wardrobe fails happen.
- Carry cash, especially for street food and local vendors.
