Jamaica Carnival is calling, and if you're planning to hit the road for **April 2025**, it's time to get all your ducks (or feathers) in a row. From the unmissable fetes to where to stay and how to get glam, here's your essential guide to making this Carnival the best one yet.

### 1. Save the Date: April 23–29, 2025
Jamaica Carnival officially kicks off on **April 23** and runs through **April 29**, with the grand finale, the **Road March**, on **Sunday, April 27**. This is the iriest Carnival on the planet, a true fusion of Soca and Dancehall.

### 2. Band Options: Xodus, GenXS, or YardMas?
Choosing your Carnival band is a key decision, and in Jamaica you've got three top-tier options:
- **Xodus Carnival**, extravagant costumes and immersive vibes.
- **GenXS Carnival**, youthful energy and attention to detail.
- **YardMas Carnival**, perfect for revellers who want to feel connected to the island's roots.

### 3. What to Pack
- Comfortable sneakers for the Road March.
- Old shoes you don't mind tossing for J'ouvert.
- Swimwear for fetes and beach vibes.
- Vitamin C and flu meds, Carnival can take a toll.
- A waterproof phone case for J'ouvert or rain.
- Sunblock, the Jamaican sun doesn't play.
- Dr. Scholl's insoles to save your feet.
- A portable battery pack.

Cards work almost everywhere, and the exchange rate is about **\$158 JMD to \$1 USD**. Keep it light, stay prepared.

### 4. Where to Stay: New Kingston is the Place to Be
- **The Jamaica Pegasus Hotel**, home to **Carnival Glam Hub**, in the heart of the Carnival route.
- **The Courtleigh Hotel & Suites**, a boutique experience with easy access to fetes.
- **AC Hotel Kingston**, stylish and modern.

**Pro Tip:** If you're booking an Airbnb, stick to New Kingston. If it's under \$100 a night, it's likely not in the best area.

### 5. Can't-Miss Fetes
- **Xodus Fete Gala** (Apr 23), kick off the week in style.
- **Soca Starter** (Apr 24), hosted by DJ Private Ryan.
- **Immortalz J'ouvert** (Apr 26), paint, powder, and unmatched vibes by GenXS.
- **Sunrise Breakfast Party** (Apr 26), Trinidad vibes in Jamaica, dress code "Pretty".
- **Duck Work** (Apr 26), cooler fete from the Scorch team.
- **Big Wall** (Apr 27), the unofficial Carnival after-party, best of dancehall.
- **Ocean X** (Apr 28), a GenXS raft-up at Maiden Cay.
- **XS Sunset** (Apr 28), GenXS's grand finale with live performances.
