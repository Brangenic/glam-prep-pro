**Rihanna's not new to Crop Over**

Rihanna has been gracing the festival with her iconic presence as far back as 2011, 2013, 2015, 2017, and 2019, notice a trend? This marks Rihanna's first Crop Over appearance since becoming a mother of two, having last attended in 2019 before the pandemic. Could this mean we'll see Queen RiRi back on the road in 2026? Only time will tell. But one thing is certain: Crop Over, or Crawpova as the locals pronounce it, is a Carnival must-do, and one that should be on every Carnival Chaser's calendar.

### Join the Fun
For six weeks, Crop Over festivities take over Barbados with Caribbean music, parades, and all-day, all-night fêtes. The rum-fuelled festival is a partying marathon featuring Bajan food fairs and arts-and-crafts markets. The biggest display of Bajan culture culminates on Grand Kadooment Day, where revellers in feathers, jewels and glitter dance to soca and calypso. So if you're thinking about attending Crop Over next year, do it. And who knows, you might even witness Queen RiRi herself. 😉
